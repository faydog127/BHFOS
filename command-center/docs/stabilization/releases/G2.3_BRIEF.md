## 7. Final phase structure and dependencies

Five independently authorized sub-releases; each has its own Decision Packet and
prerequisites. Provisioning increases monotonically and is reversible.

```
G2.3A  Foundations & Production Identity   (repository-only; no prod access)
   │      → build identity, deploy tool on main, inventories, registry, Baton/Ledger
   ▼
G2.3B  Read-only Production Diagnostics    (I2 read-only; + reconciliation gate)
   │      → PD can observe production read-only; six reconciliations resolved/attested
   ▼
G2.3C  Operator Deployment & Rollback      (I1 scoped deploy/rollback; R only)
   │      → gated behind ALL hard deployment blockers (§8) resolved/attested
   ▼
G2.3D  Controlled Production UAT           (I3 synthetic-only, Baton-gated)
   │      → dedicated test identity, registered synthetic records, cleanup
   ▼
G2.3E  Fire Drill & Low-Risk Pilot         (drill + R4A pilot; founder-burden measured)
```

**Sequencing rule:** B requires A; C requires A **and** B; D requires C; E
requires D. No phase self-authorizes the next.

**Every phase requires (all five, no exceptions):**

1. **its own child brief** (`G2.3<X>_BRIEF.md`, authored at authorization time);
2. **its own Decision Packet** (from `templates/DECISION_PACKET.template.md`);
3. **separate founder authorization** for that phase;
4. **completion evidence** recorded to the Release Ledger meeting the phase's
   evidence threshold; and
5. **satisfaction of all next-phase prerequisites** before the following phase may
   be authorized.

A phase is not "done" — and the next phase may not be authorized — until all five
are satisfied and recorded.

No exact-SHA production deployment authority is granted while any blocker above is
open and unattested.

### 8.1 Evidence-based attestation rule (binding)

A hard blocker may be resolved **by formal attestation only when** the attestation
contains sufficient evidence to establish **all** of the following:

- the **exact condition** being attested;
- the **affected system and scope**;
- the **known operational risk**;
- **why the current phase can proceed safely** despite the condition;
- the **compensating controls** in place;
- the **responsible approving authority** (the human granting the attestation);
- an **expiration or follow-up condition** where applicable.

A **bare acknowledgment, an unsupported assumption, or acceptance of an unknown
state does NOT clear a blocker.** An attestation that omits any required element
above is invalid, and the blocker remains open. Each valid attestation is recorded
in the Release Ledger (by reference, no secrets) and cited in the relevant phase's
Decision Packet.

  between chats.

  the risk of a phase, **consolidate and simplify** instead of re-asking.
